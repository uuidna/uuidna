# The school tools <Badge type="tip" text="computed in your browser" />

> Three instruments, one law: everything below runs in **your** browser from the same pure modules the tests
> hold in Node — nothing is served that could not be recomputed, and nothing you type leaves the page. Each tool
> teaches the theorem it runs on.

**Test** runs the ledger's own trial on your claim — the three controls go first
([the instrument must be able to fail](/theorem/no_audit_catches_all)), and the verdict algebra is honest:
VERIFIED means cited-and-sealed or recomputed-true; UNVERIFIED means unproven as stated, never false.

**Edit** is the film's ring ℤ/24, where undo is the ring's own law —
[`frame_ring_undo_involutive`](/theorem/frame_ring_undo_involutive): every invertible stride squares to one.

**Build** composes hexbit states on the A432 lattice and reports the whole truth of the composition: its exact
recording, its content-address, the door it enters the round by
([`door_of_the_referrer`](/theorem/door_of_the_referrer),
[`the_shift_is_the_trinity`](/theorem/the_shift_is_the_trinity)), and the beats of its steps
([`adjacent_steps_beat_at_the_tuning`](/theorem/adjacent_steps_beat_at_the_tuning)).

## Honest scope

The tester settles arithmetic and citations, never the world. The editor is the arithmetic of ℤ/24, not an NLE.
The builder reports states, doors and ratios — the composition's arithmetic, not its beauty. The pure logic lives
in [`src/quantum/apps/categories/coding/`](https://github.com/uuidna/uuidna/tree/main/src/quantum/apps/categories/coding)
and is held by Node tests identical to what your browser just ran.
