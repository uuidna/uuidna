## The sealed algebra

The two coins are not a price — they are topology: **110 − 108 = 2 = −χ of the double torus** (genus 2, χ = 2 − 2·2).
**64 = 2⁶** is the bit measure of a seal: a fixed 128 bits = 64 two-bit fold-verifications, folding O(log N)
(1024 leaves → one root in 10 levels). Contribute 2, save up to 64, keep up to 62 — and the measured saving never
goes negative. All of it decidable arithmetic, sealed in [`Coins.lean`](/lean/Coins.lean):
[`two_coins`](/theorem/two_coins) · [`contribute_two_save_sixtyfour`](/theorem/contribute_two_save_sixtyfour) ·

## Why contribute — coins as credits to the licence

Whichever angle you sail, the coins land in the same wallet the same way: **paid as credits to the
[licence](/license)** — through the voluntary **donation** (any use, a gift) or through **commercial licensing**
(the obligatory 2 per seal). One wallet, one accounting, every deposit receipted and conserved
([`two_coins`](/theorem/two_coins)).

The contribution is needed for one reason: **to compute quantum at a scale and speed worth competing with, globally
— and to prove it by measurement, not assertion.** The leverage is already sealed — contribute 2, save up to 64
([`contribute_two_save_sixtyfour`](/theorem/contribute_two_save_sixtyfour)), folding O(log N) — so every coin
compounds: credits fund the compute, the compute seals more theorems, each seal saves up to 64 more verifications.
The measure is the standard: speedups are **measured in [trials](/trials)** (never claimed), the crypto layer is
verified against **known-answer tests** (the KAT suite), and every measurement recomputes for any auditor — that is
how the work satisfies standards and competes at global scale: by being *checkable* faster than others are
*trusted*.

*Honest scope:* the sealed part is the conserved measure, the leverage bound, and the measured savings; "unimaginable
scale and speed" is the **mission** the credits fund, not a theorem — the gate would drain it if stated as one.

## The honest boundary

The coins seal **structure**: conservation, bounds, the fold. They do not prove usefulness, profit, market fairness
of the price, or the truth of your claims (the gate proves *backed*, not *authentic*) — and no theorem settles
meaning, purpose, or consequence. The mathematics is exact and anyone can recompute it; the court — human judgment —
decides what it is worth. Integrity, not truth.
