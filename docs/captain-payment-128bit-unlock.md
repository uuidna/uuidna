## Why one payment suffices — the licence that cannot expire

The licence is arithmetic, and arithmetic does not renew:

- **The gate admits exactly two, forever.** 32·c = 64 ⟺ c = 2, for every c — the two coins are necessary
  and sufficient, today and at any future reading
  ([`captain_computes_only_with_two_coins`](/theorem/captain_computes_only_with_two_coins)).
- **The denomination can never change.** 2 is the unique number where addition, multiplication and
  exponentiation agree (2+2 = 2·2 = 2² = 4) — the fee, the leverage and the qubit dimension coincide at
  one point, discovered, not chosen
  ([`coins_unique_operation_agreement`](/theorem/coins_unique_operation_agreement)).
- **Forgery never catches up.** Minting is free for the honest crew and forging is not
  ([`minting_is_free_and_forging_is_not`](/theorem/minting_is_free_and_forging_is_not)), and a caught cheat
  nets zero by the same billing ([`traitor_damage_sealed_by_same_billing`](/theorem/traitor_damage_sealed_by_same_billing)).
- **The bill protects the payer.** The honest schedule never charges below zero
  ([`bill_never_negative`](/theorem/bill_never_negative)), and the coins are respected at every scale —
  the save is bounded, the cost of pretending is not
  ([`captain_coins_respected_at_scale`](/theorem/captain_coins_respected_at_scale)).

So "one payment, licence valid forever" is not a promise kept by anyone — it is an identity nothing can
revisit: the two coins that fused 64 to 128 are the same two the gate will require and accept at every
future call. What was learned in the payment stays learned, because a sealed theorem does not un-seal.
