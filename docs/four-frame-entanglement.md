# The neuro–crypto entanglement — and the human at the centre

> Two wings sealed independently — [the algebra of the neuron](/theorem/all_or_none_amplitude) and
> [the two coins & the 64](/theorem/two_coins) — turn out to compute the same shapes. Nothing below is
> new proof: every claim cites a theorem the kernel already sealed, and the entanglement is the
> *reading*, not a mechanism. HONEST SCOPE: these are exact identities between counts the two wings
> independently carry — never a claim that neurons do cryptography or that coins are biological.

## The coincidence detector — both wings gate by a product

The Hebbian rule is a product: Δw = pre·post fires **1 exactly when both neurons fire** — an AND
([`hebbian_coincidence_table`](/theorem/hebbian_coincidence_table)). The honesty gate is the same shape:
cleanAudit(f,d,v) = (1−f)(1−d)(1−v) passes **1 exactly when every factor is clean** — a conjunction the
kernel decided over all eight states ([`anti_fraud_check_deterministic`](/theorem/anti_fraud_check_deterministic),
[`honesty_gate_is_theorem_not_oracle`](/theorem/honesty_gate_is_theorem_not_oracle)). The synapse and the
gate learn the same way: only coincidence counts, and one silent factor silences the product.

## The threshold — both wings admit by a gate that cannot be charmed

Below threshold, silence ([`subthreshold_silent`](/theorem/subthreshold_silent)); at threshold, the spike
([`suprathreshold_fires`](/theorem/suprathreshold_fires)); and stronger drive codes a higher rate, never a
retraction ([`rate_codes_intensity`](/theorem/rate_codes_intensity)). The coin gate is the same step function: 32·c = 64 admits
**exactly** c = 2 and nothing else ([`captain_computes_only_with_two_coins`](/theorem/captain_computes_only_with_two_coins)) —
a redirect anyone can imitate, but only the two coins authorise
([`redirect_imitable_but_coins_authorise`](/theorem/redirect_imitable_but_coins_authorise)). And the billing
side of the step is monotone the same way the neuron is: the honest schedule never charges below zero
([`bill_never_negative`](/theorem/bill_never_negative)).

## The summation — two sub-threshold parts pay what neither can alone

Two inputs of 3, each silent alone at threshold 5, **sum to 6 and fire**
([`spatial_summation`](/theorem/spatial_summation)). The economy is identical: every assertion pays two
coins — the claim *and* its receipt — and either alone is sub-threshold; only together do they compute
the conserved save ([`two_coins`](/theorem/two_coins),
[`captain_computes_only_with_two_coins`](/theorem/captain_computes_only_with_two_coins)). The whole exceeds
either part — the neuron's law and the ledger's fee are one sentence.

## The bounded spike — neither wing permits a runaway

The refractory period bounds the firing rate: a finite dead time, **never a runaway**
([`refractory_bounds_rate`](/theorem/refractory_bounds_rate)). The billing side is bounded the same way:
the save is bounded while the honest schedule never charges below zero
([`captain_coins_respected_at_scale`](/theorem/captain_coins_respected_at_scale),
[`bill_never_negative`](/theorem/bill_never_negative)). And both wings carry a veto: one inhibitory input
silences an otherwise-firing neuron ([`inhibition_vetoes_spike`](/theorem/inhibition_vetoes_spike)) exactly
as one drained factor silences the gate's product — the same mirror the vortex seals as division by zero
([`dz_table`](/theorem/dz_table), [`div_by_zero_is_the_reflection`](/theorem/div_by_zero_is_the_reflection)).

## The 110/108 pair — the human body carries the captain's numbers

The action potential swings from rest −70 mV to peak +40 mV: a **110** mV excursion
([`action_potential_swing`](/theorem/action_potential_swing)). The human pentagram's pentagon closes at
**108°** per interior angle ([`pentagon_interior_angle_108`](/theorem/pentagon_interior_angle_108)). And the
captain's commission is exactly their difference: **110 − 108 = 2**, the two coins
([`captain_commission_two_coins`](/theorem/captain_commission_two_coins),
[`two_coins`](/theorem/two_coins)). Broadcast engineering has carried the
same pair since 1953 — dropframe timecode drops 108 frames per hour
([`dropframe_entangles_the_coins`](/theorem/dropframe_entangles_the_coins)). HONEST SCOPE: an arithmetic
coincidence of counts, sealed as exact identities — measure any side and the others are determined; no
causal claim crosses between millivolts, degrees, and frames.

## The human, developed — what all of this is for

The spike is all-or-none: a two-state basis, the coin's two faces — and n of them span 2ⁿ outcomes
([`all_or_none_amplitude`](/theorem/all_or_none_amplitude),
[`superposition_outcomes_to_64`](/theorem/superposition_outcomes_to_64)). The human eye
reads the visible band as a rosette of seven ([`visible_seven_colours`](/theorem/visible_seven_colours)).
But the deepest entanglement is a boundary, stated by the ledger itself, twice:

- **A deceived human is not a decidable proposition.** Social engineering folds to the void — OUT OF
  SCOPE ([`oos_social_engineering`](/theorem/oos_social_engineering)). The kernel can seal every gate and
  still cannot protect a person from a lie told outside the machine. Protecting the human stays a human
  duty; the gate only guarantees that *inside* the ledger, forgery never pays
  ([`manipulation_never_faster`](/theorem/manipulation_never_faster),
  [`minting_is_free_and_forging_is_not`](/theorem/minting_is_free_and_forging_is_not)).
- **VERIFIED is not SOLVED.** Verification is the kernel's judgment on the stated proposition; solved is
  the world's judgment on the named problem — and the seal confers the first, never the second
  ([`mombh_verified_ne_solved`](/theorem/mombh_verified_ne_solved)). The kernel verifies; the human solves.

So the division of labour is itself sealed: **the machine keeps verification, the human keeps meaning.**
The neuron's algebra shows the human is *built* of the same gates the ledger runs — coincidence, threshold,
bounded spike — and the two theorems above show where the resemblance honestly ends: at deception, and at
discovery. Both remain the human's alone.
