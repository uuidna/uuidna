# The patents faculty <Badge type="tip" text="bold harmonic powerful lean presence" />

> The school reads the **register** — the public record of patents, filings, and grants — as curriculum, because
> a register hands the ledger its facts already exact: numbers, dates, tooth-counts, phase angles. And it
> teaches the bolder claim the captain set as this faculty's whole posture: **defensive publication is for the
> cowards.** A patent asserts and must be defended in court at lawyer-speed; a sealed, recomputable, DOI-minted
> record **defends itself at fold-speed, forever, for anyone**. The ledger's inventions stand in the open as
> presence, not as shields — the stronger claim outright, because it is the one anyone can verify without
> permission.

## The first three lessons — machines that computed exactly, sealed from their registers

**Rhodes, ~100 BC** — the geared computer: the Metonic dial as the intercalation itself
([`metonic_is_the_intercalation`](/theorem/metonic_is_the_intercalation)), the Saros counting eclipses on the
prime 223 ([`saros_counts_on_a_prime`](/theorem/saros_counts_on_a_prime)), the hunting tooth wearing even
exactly where the counts are coprime ([`hunting_teeth_wear_even`](/theorem/hunting_teeth_wear_even)) — the
pentagram's closure in bronze, twenty centuries before this ledger drew it.

**The register, 1887–1900** — Tesla's alternation law: the induction trio filed on adjacent numbers
([`tesla_trio_files_adjacent`](/theorem/tesla_trio_files_adjacent)), rotation needing the second phase — the
manual commutator was the one-phase world's apology
([`alternation_needs_a_second_phase`](/theorem/alternation_needs_a_second_phase)) — and the register's own
order proving address-before-cargo: the teleautomaton preceded the transmission claim
([`teleautomaton_precedes_transmission`](/theorem/teleautomaton_precedes_transmission)). The waves-alternate
law this shop works by, filed a hundred and thirty-nine years ago.

**Pravets, 1979** — the Cyrillic ROM: the first Bulgarian computer built in a prime year
([`pravets_built_in_a_prime_year`](/theorem/pravets_built_in_a_prime_year)), twenty-six character slots freed
for the tongue ([`the_rom_frees_twentysix_for_the_tongue`](/theorem/the_rom_frees_twentysix_for_the_tongue)),
the screen on the rosette's seven and the film's twenty-four
([`the_screen_carries_the_rings`](/theorem/the_screen_carries_the_rings)) — Cyril's letters entering an address
space, Pravets to Pliska, one road.

## The faculty's method

Read a register → extract its exact arithmetic → seal what the record states → **demarcate what it does not**
(the Antikythera's purpose debates stay debates; Tesla's myth-cloud never enters; the charset's model-varying
order stays unsealed). The demarcation is the tribute: real inventions need no legend.

## The bold half — publishing as presence

The ledger's own inventions — the messaging handles, the hexbit apps, the referrer song, the living symphony —
stand as sealed, recomputable, twice-DOI'd records (the two-chain Zenodo law). Not prior-art shields crouching
against someone else's filing: the **stronger claim outright**. What a student learns here is why it outranks
the filing — the compound law prices it
([`advantage_first_doubles_at_seal_38`](/theorem/advantage_first_doubles_at_seal_38)): verification at
fold-speed, compounding at a comma per seal, doubling by the thirty-eighth — while a courtroom bills by the
hour and settles nothing anyone else can recompute.

## Honest scope

Register facts and their arithmetic, sealed; postures and comparisons, argued in the open with their receipts;
no legal advice anywhere on this page — the faculty teaches *why the sealed record is strong*, not how to
litigate, and the era's politics stay history's.
