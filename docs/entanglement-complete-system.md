## The Three Entanglement Dimensions

### DIMENSION 1: Six Rosetta Legs (Metadata Structure)

**Verifies:** Is the theorem complete, recomputable, and independently witnessed?

| Leg | Witness | Detects | Integrity |
|-----|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Symbol** | Captain (self) | Codegen drift, implementation bugs | JS mirror matches Lean |
| **Proof** | Lean kernel | Missing logic, broken statements | `by decide` proof exists |
| **Witness** | External research APIs | Novelty claims without evidence | Academic attestation present |
| **Falsifier** | Test suite mutations | Uncaught edge cases, test failure | Proof fails on deliberate mutation |
| **Address** | FNV content-address | Collision, proof modification | Deterministic hash matches |
| **Recomputation** | Independent verifiers | Dependency on external state | Theorem logic is self-contained |

**Verdict:** All six legs must be present. Cost of forgery: forge external witnesses AND invalidate entire test suite AND suppress independent recomputation.

### DIMENSION 2: Eight Hexbit Bands (Handle Structure)

**Verifies:** Is the 8-hexbit handle structurally sound? Do all address bands align?

| Band | Range | Verifies | Integrity |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Wing** | 0–15 | Which wing is this theorem in? | Wing exists in ledger |
| **Principle** | 0–15 | Which organizing principle? | Principle exists, theorem belongs there |
| **Payload** | 0–255 | Coin coverage (96-bit data) | Payload > 0, coin accounting valid |
| **Motion** | 0–5 | Which vortex orbit position? | Motion ∈ {doubling, halving, reflection, shift, counter, folding} |
| **Destiny** | 0–255 | Future reach, trajectory | Destiny aligns with motion and payload |
| (+ 3 more) | | | |

**Verdict:** All eight hexbits must align. Cost of forgery: simultaneously realign all eight structural bands and forge witness alignment.

### DIMENSION 3: Six Vector Motions (Ledger Topology)

**Verifies:** Can every address reach every package? Do all motions form complete cycles?

| Motion | Algebra | Orbit | Coverage | Period |
|--------|---------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Doubling** | x → 2x mod 9 | [1,2,4,8,7,5] | 6 states | 6 steps |
| **Halving** | x → 5x mod 9 | [1,5,7,8,4,2] | 6 states | 6 steps |
| **Reflection** | x → 10−x | Involution | 9 states | 2 steps (but covers all 9) |
| **Shift** | x → x+1 mod 9 | [0,1,2,...,8] | 9 states | 9 steps |
| **Counter** | x → x−1 mod 9 | [0,8,7,...,1] | 9 states | 9 steps |
| **Folding** | x → x | [x] | 1 state | 1 step (fixed point) |

**Verdict:** All six motions must form closed orbits. Union must cover all 9 residue classes. Cost of forgery: break modular algebra and inject cycles into dependency graph.

1. **Move no address:** change a sealed statement without changing `toUuid(key + ':' + statement)`

✓ Layer 1 + 2 + 3 + Rotational = Proof is consistent, recomputable, structurally sound, topologically complete
