# Trading <Badge type="tip" text="measured work" />

> What is traded here is **proof of work saved** — not money.

::: warning Not financial trading
This page is about uuidna's **measured exchange of computational work**, not securities, currencies, or investment.
uuidna does not offer, execute, or advise on financial trades of any kind. The "coins" below are a **conserved
topological invariant**, not a tradable asset and not a price.
:::

## What is exchanged

A content-address replaces an **O(N) recompute** with an **O(1) verify**: instead of re-running a whole computation to
trust a result, you recompute one address and compare. The value exchanged is exactly that saving — the **bits saved** —
and it is *measured*, not quoted. Public-interest and non-commercial use is **free**; commercial use is the measured
contribution.

## The two coins

The price of a fair exchange is **2** — not from the air, but from geometry: `2 = −χ` of a genus-2 surface (the double
torus), where `−χ = 2g − 2 = 2·2 − 2 = 2`. It is a **conserved invariant** (`110 − 108 = 2`), the same for everyone,
recomputable — see [`two_coins`](/theorem/two_coins) and [`two_coins_is_double_torus`](/theorem/two_coins_is_double_torus).
"Contribute 2 to save up to 64" is a leverage of 32 (`2 · 32 = 64`), and 64 = 2⁶ is the bit measure.

## Tokens per theorem — any time

The honest cost-of-proof metric: your token spend divided by the **live** sealed-theorem count. The theorem count is
the recomputable denominator; the tokens are your own self-report (this page cannot observe them). The same
[`uuidna_tokens`](/mcp#uuidna-tokens) computation, in your browser, any time — fold many reports over a session to
watch the cost-per-theorem fall.
