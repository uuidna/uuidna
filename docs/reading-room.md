# The reading room <Badge type="tip" text="nothing pasted leaves the page" />

> The books the first thread surfaced — music, dance, singing — asked for a room. This is it: paste any
> public-domain passage and it becomes a first-class ledger object **in your browser** — its recomputable
> identity, its facts decided by the ear that can refuse (truth and falsehood wear different verdicts —
> [`silence_never_refutes`](/theorem/silence_never_refutes) guards what stays open), and its quotes standing the
> citation trial, each leaving **addressed** whatever the verdict. An unverified quote with an address is a door
> for the [open questions](/open-questions).

<BookRoom />

## Honest scope

Identity, arithmetic and citations — never meaning, never attribution, never taste. The ear hears sums in digits
and in words; what it cannot hear excludes nothing. The pure logic lives in
[`src/quantum/apps/categories/books/`](https://github.com/uuidna/uuidna/tree/main/src/quantum/apps/categories/books)
and is held by the same tests in Node that your browser just reran.
