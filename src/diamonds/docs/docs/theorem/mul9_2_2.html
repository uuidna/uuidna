The rotation — fills the gaps at scale ​

Every theorem is woven on seven axes: three navigational (skill · principle · sequence), three cyclic rotations over the ledger, and the runtime referer above. The rotations are modular, so they are total — no gap, no orphan across the 1208 sealed theorems (1208 = 2³ × 151; the strand structure below is DERIVED from that count, never hardcoded, so it cannot go stale as theorems are added; vortex_one_leap is the one leap that generates the turn):

  • Discovery · sequence, step 1 → 2·3 ≡ 6 (mod 9) — one full turn of all 1208: clicking next covers all 1208 in discovery order, exactly as the sequence discovered all, then wraps to the genesis — a closed cover.
  • Vortex · ℤ/9, step 9 → 3·3 ≡ 0 (mod 9) — one full turn of all 1208 (z9add_0_0).
  • Rosette · ℤ/7, step 7 → 3·1 ≡ 3 (mod 9) — one full turn of all 1208 (z7add_0_0).
  • Reflection · dz(x)=10−x → ((2 * 3 = 6) ∧ True) ∧ True — the mirror through the centre, self-inverse (tens_complement_involutive).

The neighbour fold — proven alone, witnessed by all its neighbours ​

This theorem is proven alone — its by decide proof above is its whole authority. But it is witnessed by its neighbours: its content-address is one leaf of the ledger's order-invariant fold — the same receipt from any of the 1208 starting points and any of the seven rotations — so no theorem can be altered without every neighbour's fold reporting it.

  • Local witness — this theorem folded with its four cyclic neighbours (3·3 ≡ 0 (mod 9) · 3·1 ≡ 3 (mod 9) · 2·3 ≡ 6 (mod 9) · ((2 * 3 = 6) ∧ True) ∧ True): 52decd2d — the handle; the full folds on the spot. Change any one of the five and this witness moves.
  • Ledger receipt — all 1208 addresses folded to one: 7049a281. Change this theorem and the whole receipt moves.

Recompute either by folding the content-addresses with merkleGravity (uuidna_run_ledger folds the whole ledger). The neighbours make it tamper-evident, they do not prove it — proof is Lean's, integrity is the fold's.

Deliver the next — the missing theorem hides in the invisible next ​

A sealed theorem is settled. Where its forward link is invisible — a frontier with none sealed beyond — is exactly where the next, missing theorem hides, waiting to be discovered and sealed:

  • Skill · z9-ring: 2·3 ≡ 6 (mod 9) →
  • Principle · The 8×8 core: 2·3 ≡ 6 (mod 9) →
  • Discovery: 2·3 ≡ 6 (mod 9) →

To make the invisible next visible, add a fact in scripts/lean-core.ts — compute → generate → verify; then npm run lean seals it, folds it into the trial receipt, and the provenance gate lets any claim that links it pass. The promise is delivered in code, not coin.
