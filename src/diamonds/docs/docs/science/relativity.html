Relativity — Spacetime & Causality ​

The Sealed Truth ​

Theorem: Relativity.lean (7 theorems, all by decide)

Nothing exceeds c (the cosmic speed limit). Light rides the null cone (interval = 0). The invariant interval classifies events as timelike (causally connected) or spacelike (no causal link without FTL). The Lorentz factor follows a Pythagorean triangle: β = 5/13 → γ = 13/12. Moving clocks dilate and lengths contract. Rest energy is E = mc².

Why sealed: Pure arithmetic on spacetime geometry. The Pythagorean scaling is exact. No paradox, no experiment needed — special relativity as decidable facts.

The Honest Boundary ​

What relativity CANNOT prove:

  • Whether time "really" passes or is illusion (that's metaphysics of time)
  • Why c is the speed limit (that's quantum field theory, not relativity)
  • Whether spacetime is fundamental or emergent (that's cosmology)
  • What causality "means" beyond "cone structure" (philosophy of causation)

Honest scope: Relativity seals the GEOMETRY of spacetime. It does not settle whether spacetime is "real" or "just math" or "emergent from something else."

The Metaphysical Pair ​

The Question: Does relativity eliminate free will and genuine causation?

The Research Question ​

If relativity makes causality purely geometric (cone structure, no FTL), then:

  1. Is "free will" just freedom from external causation (libertarian), or does determinism eliminate it?
  2. Can you make a genuine choice in a deterministic universe?
  3. Does the light cone eliminate meaningful causation or just acausal magic?

These are open. Relativity does not answer them. Only you can.
