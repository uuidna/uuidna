# @uuidna/research Documentation

Deep research framework: recomputable research through the imprint codec, corroboration scans, book/movie/record/standard audits, publication composition, the reporter's method, prior art anchoring, and forward-chaining reasoning.

## Modules

- **Deep Research** (`deepResearch`) — execute multi-dimensional research audits with corroboration requirements
- **Publication Pipeline** (`composePublication`, `auditPublication`, `revisePublication`) — seal research findings as publications with theorem citations and gating
- **Content Audits** (`auditBook`, `auditMovie`, `auditStandard`, `auditCve`) — prove provenance and integrity of published works
- **Research Evidence** (`corroborate`, `researchEvidence`) — cross-reference findings with ≥2 independent sources
- **Theorem Linking** (`extractDecidable`, `linkBookFacts`) — bind discovered facts to the sealed ledger
- **Vocabulary & Terms** (`vocabulary`, `nistConstant`) — canonical terminology and NIST constant anchors
- **Reasoning** (`reason`, `reflects`, `priorArt`) — forward-chaining derivations and reflection

## Honest Scope

Research here extracts **PROVENANCE and STRUCTURE, never meaning**. A publication publishes only when:
- Honesty gate clears (theorem citations verify)
- Corroboration holds (≥2 independent sources)
- Content addresses are recomputable (order-invariant folds)

Integrity is provable. Truth is not. Research here proves what was published and who published it, never whether it is true in the world.
